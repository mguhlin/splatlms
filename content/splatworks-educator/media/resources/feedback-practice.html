Feedback practice: turn a comment into a revision

The following examples use fictional practice data. The question is which location might suit a young plant on a warm day.

Response A

The tree place is best because it is nice. My graph has green bars.

Response B

I would investigate the tree-shaded location first. The sample lists 25°C there and 34°C on the sunny paved site. A cooler location may help, but these four fictional records do not prove which site is best for a particular plant. We would also need water, light and repeated observations.

Your task

  1. Name one strength in A.
  2. Choose the most important missing criterion: accurate evidence, reasoning or communication.
  3. Write one actionable next step.
  4. Revise A so the next action has a visible result.
  5. Decide what new question would check transfer.

One possible feedback message

Your claim identifies a location. Add two temperature values from the sample and explain why that comparison supports investigating the location. Then state one limitation.

Do not require every learner’s revision to match Response B word for word. Judge the evidence and reasoning, including a defensible different recommendation.
