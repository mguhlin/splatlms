SplatWorks Educator capstone checklist

For each criterion mark Ready, Revise or Not yet. Repair every Not yet before a classroom pilot. This self-check does not create a graded LMS submission.

CriterionReady evidence
AlignmentA specific subject/grade goal, local standard and observable success criteria align with the final task.
Modeling and scaffoldingA complete worked example, a partly supported attempt and a fresh independent problem are included.
Instructional evidenceThe plan explains which strategy is used and what learner action makes it visible; no app effect is invented.
AssessmentEvery learner produces evidence; an error pattern leads to a named next teaching action.
Feedback and revisionA criterion-based next step is followed by time to revise and a visible change.
Access and suitabilityCaptions/text alternatives, readable materials, language supports and a non-timed/low-tech route fit the learners.
Tools and filesA small purposeful toolchain works; projects and exports reopen; links and permissions are checked.
Game decisionA game is omitted or has a clear purpose, previewed content, short timing, alternative and debrief.
TransferA later or changed task checks the intended learning beyond copying, teamwork or a game score.

After teaching

Compare initial and final responses using the same criteria. Describe the change in the reasoning, any learners still needing support and the next adjustment. A completion badge does not establish external accreditation or a measured student-learning effect.
