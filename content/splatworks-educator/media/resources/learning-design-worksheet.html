Learning design worksheet

Copy these prompts into your preferred editor or print this page.

  1. Subject, grade band, current local standard and prerequisite knowledge.
  2. Learning intention: learners will be able to do what, using which content?
  3. Two or three observable success criteria.
  4. Likely misconception and an observation or example that could reveal it.
  5. Worked example: teacher action, reasoning to say aloud and student response.
  6. Guided task: scaffold to provide and the step learners must supply.
  7. Independent new case: what changes and what thinking stays the same?
  8. Tool choices: thinking/modeling, product/evidence and feedback.
  9. Evidence to collect from every learner and the next teaching action for a common error.
  10. Access choices, language supports, extension and a paper fallback.
  11. Editable source, shareable export and the result of a reopen test.
  12. Later retrieval/transfer prompt and when it will be used.

Plan–monitor–evaluate

Before: What is the goal and first action? During: Which criterion is met and what is missing? After: What improved, what strategy helped and what will change next time?
