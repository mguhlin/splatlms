Ecosystem field guide: all 68 destinations

Reviewed October 31, 2026. The live Studio registry contains 68 destinations and matches the local source used for the course screenshots. This is a complete orientation map, not a requirement to use every tool in one lesson. Descriptions below are original instructional suggestions. Product features and availability can change.

Capabilities inside the whiteboard

The Studio inventory lists public destinations, not every internal command. Explore text, pen, shapes, sticky notes, selection/grouping, panels/pages, images, templates/backgrounds and relevant advanced options such as connectors, comments and audio notes. Graphs, picture graphs, concept representations and classroom widgets can support a task when they expose the intended thinking. Preview the exact controls and save/export workflow in your installation. District collaboration and turn-in workflows need the school configuration; do not assume they are active on a browser-only board.
