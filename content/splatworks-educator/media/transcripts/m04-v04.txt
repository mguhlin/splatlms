Walkthrough: retrieve, respond and revise

Prepare the cycle
Prepare five questions for the same goal you have developed through the course. Write the answer and a brief explanation for each. Define three observable criteria for the final product. Use QuizSplat and RubricSplat if they fit, or use the supplied worksheet. The goal is a coherent cycle of evidence and response, not a collection of unrelated quizzes.

Work a sample response
Read the two supplied sample explanations. Identify one strength in the weaker response and one important next action. Revise the answer yourself using that action. Check whether the revision improves the reasoning rather than only changing the wording. This rehearsal helps you discover whether your feedback is specific enough for a learner to use.

Plan the later check
Schedule a related prompt two days later. Change the example while retaining the thinking demand so that the learner cannot simply reproduce the original sentence. Write the result that would lead you to reteach, provide additional practice or offer an extension. Your deliverable is the question set, criteria, visible revision and follow-up plan. Use the module quiz to check your own reasoning.
