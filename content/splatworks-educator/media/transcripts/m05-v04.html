Capstone: bring the ecosystem into your classroom

Open the captioned video · WebVTT captions

Build a coherent lesson

  • Goal and criteria
  • Model, guided attempt, new task
  • Feedback and revision

Assemble a forty to fifty minute lesson for your own grade band and subject. State the local content standard you already teach, the goal and two or three success criteria. Include a worked example, a partly supported attempt and a new independent response. Select a small toolchain that serves those stages. You do not need every app in the lesson; the course field guide remains available when another need appears.

Test the learner experience

  • Reopen project and exports
  • Check links, captions and alternatives
  • Collect evidence from every learner

Rehearse the lesson with an anonymous sample. Reopen the source and exported files. Check links, captions, reading order and a workable paper or low-tech alternative. If you include a game station, name its purpose and collect a debrief. Plan an individual response so that the evidence does not depend only on a group product or a game score.

Review, revise and transfer

  • Use the capstone checklist
  • Repair every not-yet criterion
  • Compare initial and final reasoning

Use the capstone checklist to review the lesson and repair every criterion marked not yet. After teaching, compare initial and final explanations against the same criteria. Describe what changed in the reasoning and what you will change next time. Finish the course’s five knowledge checks and lesson steps in the LMS. The badge marks course completion; approved credit or external certification requires the provider’s separate authorization.
